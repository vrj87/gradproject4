**Title:** Google Photos — Photo you could not find!  
**Intro:** Think of **one** photo you knew was in Google Photos and still could not find the first time you looked. Stay on that photo for every question.

This is the **canonical** Part 3 study. Written replies **are** the interviews for this project. Count a row toward n ≥ 5 when it passes the table below.

---

**G1. How long has Google Photos been your primary photo library?**

- Under 12 months
- 12 months to 3 years
- More than 3 years
- Never

**G2. When you need an old photo, what do you usually do first?** (required)

- I search (words, people, places)
- I open Memories / albums
- I scroll the timeline until I see it
- I ask someone else / use another phone

**G3. Can you recall a photo you knew was in Google Photos but failed to find the first time?** (required)

- Yes
- No

**G4. Was that photo from a trip, holiday, wedding, concert, or “that place” — you remember the vibe, not the date or town?** (required)

- Yes — trip / event / that place
- No — it was a screenshot, receipt, medicine, or document
- No — it was a face I know but cannot name
- No — years of mixed photos I cannot scroll
- No — it lives on someone else’s phone

**G5. The photo you could not find was closest to…** (required)

- A trip, holiday, wedding, concert, or “that café / that place” — vibe remembered, date or town forgotten
- Screenshot, receipt, medicine, ticket, document
- A face I know, not a name I could search
- Years of mixed photos, no specific trip
- I cannot think of a photo I failed to find

**G6. When you started looking, what did you still have in your head?** (required)

- How the place felt (small café, beach, hotel, lights, yellow chairs)
- That it was a trip / holiday / wedding / concert
- Someone I was with — face, not a search name
- An object (food, a sign, clothes, a bottle)
- What was happening (after the swim, dinner, that night)
- How it felt (happy, rushed, “that night”)
- A rough time (“last year”, winter) — not the date
- Almost nothing except that the photo exists

**G7. What did you try first in Google Photos?** (required)

- Search box
- Memories
- An album I thought it lived in
- Scrolling the timeline
- I skipped Photos (person / other phone)
- I did not know where to start

**G8. If Photos could take one thing you already remembered, what should it take?** (required)

- How the place felt
- The trip or event, without the town name
- An object plus what was happening
- A face I know
- A rough time (“that winter”, “last year”)
- A photo that looks like one I can point to

**G9. How did this story end?** (required)

- I still have not found it
- I found it by scrolling, asking someone, or another app — not by search
- I found it after a second search, still from a vague memory
- I remembered the exact date, album, place name, or filename, and then search worked
- Not sure
