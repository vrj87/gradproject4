## Qualify (must all be true)

- Google Photos has been the **primary** library for ≥ 12 months
- Can recall **at least one** photo they knew existed but **failed to find on the first search**
- The failed find started from **incomplete memory** (not date / album / filename)
- **S2** trip / event / place vibe — not screenshot, medicine, or known-item

In-segment n must reach **≥ 5** form rows before Phase 4 locks.
