## 0:00–4:00 · Qualify (Q1–Q3)

If they fail here, thank them and stop. Do not count them.

**Q1. How long has Google Photos been where you keep most of your photos?**

- Less than 12 months → end
- 12 months to 3 years
- More than 3 years
- I work at Google / Photos, or someone close to me does → end

**Q2. When you need an old photo, what do you usually do first?**

- Type something in Search
- Open Memories or an album
- Scroll the library until I see it — that usually works → end if this always works on a small library
- Ask someone else or check another phone

**Q3. The photo you could not find was closest to…**

- A trip, holiday, wedding, concert, or “that café / that place” — vibe remembered, date or town forgotten → **S2, continue**
- Screenshot, receipt, medicine, ticket, document → end (not this study)
- A face I know, not a name I could search → end
- Years of mixed photos, no specific trip → end
- I cannot think of a photo I failed to find → end

## 4:00–12:00 · The photo, remembered, forgotten (Q4–Q6)

**Q4. In one sentence: what was in that photo, and roughly when in your life — not the calendar date if you don’t have it?**

Write their sentence. That is the Phase 6 task.

**Q5. When you started looking, what did you still have in your head?**  
(Read the list only if they stall. Tick all they mention.)

- How the place felt (small café, beach, hotel, lights, yellow chairs)
- That it was a trip / holiday / wedding / concert
- Someone I was with — face, not a search name
- An object (food, a sign, clothes, a bottle)
- What was happening (after the swim, dinner, that night)
- How it felt (happy, rushed, “that night”)
- A rough time (“last year”, winter) — not the date
- Almost nothing except that the photo exists

Their words: ________________________________

**Q6. What had gone — the bit you kept reaching for?**

- The exact date
- The album name
- The town, hotel, or venue name
- The words I would normally type
- The filename
- I actually remembered a precise keyword → **known-item, do not count**

Their words: ________________________________

Verbatim query: ________________________________

**Q9. What came back?**

- Nothing useful
- Other photos from the same trip — not the one I meant
- The right kind of place, wrong trip or year

- Felt slow / generally bad even for photos I can name → generic search, not our bet
- I never looked at results — I scrolled

**Q10. Do you think the photo you wanted was actually in that set? Why?**

- I could not tell
- I did not get a result set → formulate / never searched

## 20:00–27:00 · After the miss (Q11–Q13)

**Q11. After that miss, how did you try to get the photo?**

- Kept scrolling the timeline
- Tried a different search later
- Opened another phone or laptop
- Asked a partner, friend, or family
- Looked in another app (WhatsApp, Files, Apple Photos, Drive…)
- Gave up — I still do not have it

**Q12. The next attempt — what happened?**

- I used a new clue and found it
- I searched again the same way and hit the same miss
- I stopped searching and scrolled
- I left Photos
- I have not tried again
- It only worked once I remembered the exact date, album, or place name → do not count

**Q13. If the right photo had been sitting among near-misses, what would have helped you say “that’s the one”?**

- Grouped by trip or day
- Grouped by how the place felt
- Faces I know, even without names
- A line in my own words
- Two or three “is this it?”
- Nothing — I would still open each one
- It was never going to be in that set

## 27:00–30:00 · Close (Q14–Q15)

**Q14. If Photos could take one thing you already remembered, what should it take?**  
Wait. Do not say “better search.”

- How the place felt
- The trip or event, without the town name
- An object plus what was happening
- A face I know
- A rough time (“that winter”, “last year”)
- A photo that looks like one I can point to
- Something else: ________

**Q15. How did this story end?**

- I still have not found it
- I found it by scrolling, asking someone, or another app — not by search
- I found it after a second search, still from a vague memory
- I remembered the exact date, album, place name, or filename, and then search worked → do not count
- Not sure

Disconfirm if you still have a minute: “Was it slowness, or that you didn’t have the word search wanted?” / “If you had remembered the date, would this have been easy?”
