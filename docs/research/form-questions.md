# 15 optional live probes

Fellowship Part 3 **study** is the Google Form (G1–G9).  
Live form: [https://forms.gle/wvQ3pQVQLhxMDFTm9](https://forms.gle/wvQ3pQVQLhxMDFTm9).

These 15 are optional if you also hop on a call. They do not replace the form.

1. How long has Google Photos been where you keep most of your photos?
2. When you need an old photo, what do you usually do first?
3. The photo you could not find was closest to… (keep only trip / “that place”)
4. In one sentence: what was in that photo, and roughly when in your life?
5. When you started looking, what did you still have in your head?
6. What had gone?
7. What did you try first in Google Photos?
8. If you searched, what did the words look like? What did you type?
9. What came back?
10. Do you think the photo you wanted was actually in that set?
11. After that miss, how did you try to get the photo?
12. The next attempt — what happened?
13. If it had been among near-misses, what would have helped you recognize it?
14. If Photos could take one thing you already remembered, what should it take?
15. How did this story end?
